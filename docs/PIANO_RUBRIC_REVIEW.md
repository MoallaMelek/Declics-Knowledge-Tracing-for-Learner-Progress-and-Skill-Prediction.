# Piano pilot: exact teacher-review materials

Review status: pending. No teacher has approved this rubric, exercise catalogue, ordinal margin or future target mapping. The live machine-readable copy is GET /api/practice/catalog, version piano-pilot-v1. The practice export includes the full rubric and catalogue used for its observations.

| Candidate dimension | Guided task | Needs work | Developing | Comfortable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pitch | Five-note ascending/descending phrase | Several unintended notes | Most intended notes, isolated errors | Intended notes throughout this attempt |
| Rhythm | Eight quarter notes on one key | Unsteady pulse or values | Mostly steady, some deviations | Steady quarter notes throughout |
| Duration | Four sustained notes | Releases substantially early | Approximate intended duration | Holds each intended duration |
| Articulation | Same phrase connected then detached | Difference not discernible | Audible but inconsistent distinction | Consistent connected/detached distinction |
| Fingering | Five-finger position, 1-2-3-4-5 | Repeated substitutions | Isolated substitutions | Intended fingering through attempt |
| Score reading | Original four-note motif | Most notes differ from reference | Mostly corresponds, hesitations | Intended note order in this attempt |

These are qualitative draft descriptors, not fixed cents/milliseconds thresholds or psychometric mastery. Duration and score_reading are deliberately distinct pilot categories, not silently mapped onto saved model concepts. Tempo consistency is currently considered in the provisional rhythm task, without a newly trained tempo model. Technique, posture and quality require separate judgments and remain outside this notebook rubric.

Teacher review questions: Are the tasks suitable for the intended beginner population? Are the descriptors distinguishable and fair? Which errors are acceptable at each level? Is the reference score sufficient? Should tempo and rhythm be separate? What constitutes a comparable repeat? Should different teacher evaluations be pooled? What independent adjudication is needed before any binary target exists?

Review record to prepare outside the public dataset: reviewer role, review date, each accepted/revised/rejected descriptor, exercise difficulty and prerequisites if explicitly approved, rationale, rubric revision, adjudication protocol. Do not put student identities in this record or repository. Publish a new immutable rubric version after review; keep earlier observations tied to their original version. No approval control is exposed in this application.

Before institutional or minor-participant collection, the responsible teacher and institution must establish the actual participation, guardian/learner process where applicable, access, withdrawal, retention and export rules. The delivered app is a single-user local notebook, not an approved research recruitment or school record service. Use demonstration mode for fictional training/testing examples.

Prospective collection proposal: stable pseudonyms, repeated comparable exercises over several weeks, logged timestamps/source/rubric, independent second ratings on a subset, disagreement analysis, and a learner-disjoint evaluation plan. Four learners only permits a mechanically nonempty split; it does not establish statistical adequacy. Determine cohort size from the intended analysis and uncertainty before claiming population results.
