# Phase 7 - executive summary

DÉCLIC O08 uses previous musical practice outcomes to summarize evidence-supported difficulties
and predict a subsequent performance. Phase 7 preserves the verified Phase 6 chronological safeguards,
active neural models and piano-domain abstention.

Thirty-six new CPU neural training runs completed: 33 predefined representation comparisons and
three exploratory Yousician metadata extensions. The work adds regularized logistic, PFA-style,
Ridge and boosted-tree research controls, validation-only temperature scaling, missing-history stress
tests, five-fold learner-grouped AMME evaluation and additional Yousician selection diagnostics.

Across the main three splits, validation-selected SingPAD networks reached mean test log-loss
0.517925 versus 0.524188 for validation-selected controls. AMME and Yousician did not demonstrate
average neural superiority. Extra Yousician history metadata did not provide convincing average gains.
Calibration improved some cases and worsened others. Splits previously examined in Phase 6 remain
exploratory evidence, not an independent confirmatory cohort.

The new demonstration uses real held-out histories and separates history delivery, actual neural
inference and signed-receipt outcome reveal. It exposes errors rather than selecting flattering cases.
Observed performance, descriptive states, model predictions and educational interpretation remain
separate. Recommendations use only known historical exercises and make no educational-effect claim.

A local opt-in collection ledger provides canonical events, pseudonyms, consent metadata, idempotency,
server receipt times, immutable future-attempt plans, export, deletion, retention and access controls.
Live field predictions abstain until the measurement contract and target-domain model are validated.
The teacher-review workflow contains no approved piano mapping. The service remains a local demo,
not a certified secure multi-user deployment.

The remaining external priorities are qualified musical review, agreement on teammate assessment
contracts, a consented prospective pilot and an untouched confirmatory cohort. Full pedagogical and
field validation are explicitly incomplete. Reports, actual metrics, tests, source fingerprints and
Git/CI evidence accompany the delivered engineering work.
